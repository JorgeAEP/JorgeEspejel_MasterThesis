# *Fusarium graminearum* curation audit guide

`selected_correction_reaction_and_medium_audit.csv` is the final
reaction-level curation record for the corrected derived model. It is paired
with `final_validation_condition_results.csv`,
`biomass_precursor_producibility.csv` and the final correction summary. These
files document the selected edits and the validation conditions used before
the corrected model was saved.

Scripts 15 and 21 provide the supporting copy-only diagnostic and validation
steps used by the final model-creation Script 22.
