# *Bipolaris sorokiniana* curation audit guide

`selected_reaction_before_after_audit.csv` is the final reaction-level audit
for the saved corrected model. It records the selected stoichiometric edits to
`UF00430_CR` and `UF00435_C`, including before/after equations and balance
information. `old_C_explicit_H_neutralization_edit_audit.csv` records the
associated background curation. The validation, precursor-producibility and
checksum tables document that the final derived SBML was written after the
selected correction passed the recorded tests.

The earlier exploratory and copy-only tests were intentionally not collapsed
into a second derived model. They are represented by the archived support
Scripts 67 and 73 and by the final selected-correction records retained here.
